## What to leave out

This is the important half of the template.

- **Any button whose name explains it.** No "Save Project — saves the project."
- **Anything the reader would do without being told.** They can find a Cancel button.
- **Esri's full option list.** Cover the path that works; link out for the rest.
- **Groups and tab locations for their own sake.** Say where a button is because the reader
  needs to find it, not to document the ribbon.

The page earns its keep on two things only: **when you'd want this** and **what silently
goes wrong**. Everything else is filler.
